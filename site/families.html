Other families of Galway

The lords who founded the town, the Gaelic families around it, and townspeople who outlasted many of the tribes.

de Burgh (Burke) · Druid Lane Hall of the Red Earl Richard de Burgh was born in County Limerick around 1193 to William de Burgh of Norfolk and a daughter Donál Mór Ó Brian, King of Tuamhain.
Fallon · High Street The Fallons of High Street The Fallons were never among the Tribes of Galway but unlike so many of them they demonstrated remarkable longevity within the town.
Nolan · High Street The Nolans of High Street Writing at Galway on 4 April 1616, Nicholas Blake wrote to his son John saying that his letter of 25 February arrived in Galway on 14 March.
Ó hAllmhuráin · Quay Lane Ó hAllmhuráin of Quay Lane As of November 1724, Andrew Holleran was a Catholic living in a cabin with one chimney on Quay Lane in Saint Nicholas’s parish.

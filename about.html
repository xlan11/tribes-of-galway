About the Tribes of Galway Experience

A guide to the fourteen families of Galway and the town they built, site by site.

Where the histories come from

Every site on this website is drawn from “The Tribes Experience of Galway & other notable families”, researched and written by Adrian Martyn and edited by Pete Kelly.
